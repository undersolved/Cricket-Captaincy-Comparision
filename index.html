How each captain played it

Team selection, bowling plans and risk-taking changed across two decades.

Conditions that shaped the tactics

Night dew

Wet balls are hard for spinners to grip. Dhoni preferred chasing under dew; Rohit set high first-innings totals instead.

Pace and pitches

Dhoni leaned on spin at home. Kohli and Rohit built seam attacks that could take 20 wickets anywhere.

Rising par scores

250 was defendable in Dhoni's ODIs. Modern par is past 320, so Rohit pushed the early run rate higher.

Knockout games

Dhoni won 3 ICC trophies with calm finishes. Rohit paired a 72.52% overall win rate with the 2024 T20 World Cup.
